# Adriel M. Sierra

## Postdoctoral Researcher | Plant Ecology & Urban Microbiome

About

My research integrates field-based ecology, multi-omics techniques, and bioinformatics across three thematic axes to understand biodiversity dynamics and its ecological role in the conservation of natural, agricultural, and urban ecosystems.

**Plant landscape dynamics under land-use change.** 

How do habitat loss and fragmentation impact biodiversity across genetic, species, and ecosystem levels? 

I study metapopulation and metacommunity frameworks to evaluate how life-history traits govern spatial and temporal colonization–extinction dynamics in Amazonian leaf-inhabiting bryophytes and associated microbiome.

**Plant–microbial functional ecology.** 

What mechanisms drive reciprocal host-microbiome interactions, and what are their consequences for plant adaptation and chemical defense? 

This axis seeks to identify candidate microbes with beneficial effects on the host and elucidate the mechanisms through which they work, bridging fundamental plant microbial ecology with applied solutions for plant resilience.

**Microbiome-inspired urban interventions.** 

How can urban landscapes be designed to promote beneficial microbial communities that enhance ecosystem services and human health? 

By bridging landscape ecology and plant–microbe interactions, this axis seeks to establish scalable urban planning and indoor green interventions that enhance human health and well-being.
